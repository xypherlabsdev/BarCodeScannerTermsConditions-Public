2.The app

QRScan Pro is a free, ad-supported app for Android that lets you:

  • scan QR codes and barcodes with the camera or from an image, one at a time or several in a row (batch scan);
  • create QR codes for links, text, Wi-Fi, contacts, email and phone numbers, with your own colours and shapes, and barcodes in EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 128, Code 39 and ITF;
  • keep a History of scanned and created codes on your device, search it and mark favourites;
  • open, copy, share and save codes, join Wi-Fi networks from Wi-Fi codes, search the web for a product barcode, and see hints about risky links;
  • start a scan from a home-screen widget.

The app currently has no in-app purchases. We may add, change or remove features at any time.

5.Scanned content and safety hints

A code can lead anywhere. We do not create, check or control the content behind codes you scan, including links, Wi-Fi networks, contacts and payment (UPI) codes.

  • The app's link safety hints are automatic checks done on your device. They can miss a dangerous link or warn about a safe one, and they are not a security service.
  • Always check the payee name and amount in your payment app before you pay with a scanned code.
  • If you turn on Auto-open URLs in Settings, scanned links open without a second look. Use it with care.

What you do with scanned content is your decision and your responsibility.
